About the College

Siddaganga Institute of Technology, Tumkur, popularly known as SIT, was established in the year 1963. It was founded and nurtured by His Holiness Dr. Sree Sree Shivakumara Swamiji. The present president of Sree Siddaganga Education Society, Sree Sree Siddalinga Swamiji, is providing directional guidelines. SIT is one among 125 institutions run by Sree Siddaganga Education Society. The institute currently has more than 4500 students pursuing their studies across 14 undergraduate programmes and 06 post graduate programmes. The institute has 15 departments recognized as Research Centres by Visvesvaraya Technological University, Belgaum, with 187 Research scholars pursuing Ph.D. programmes. The institute boasts 290 experienced, qualified, and devoted faculty members, with 155 holding Ph.D. degrees from prestigious institutions like IISc Bangalore, IIT Madras, IIT Bombay, IIT Kharagpur, IIT Guwahati, etc. Another 49 faculty members are pursuing Ph.D. programmes at various reputed institutions. Faculty members have completed 60 projects with a grant of Rs.10 Crore, with 33 ongoing projects worth Rs.8 Crore, funded by organizations like DRDO, DST, ISRO, AICTE, and VGST

Department of Computer Science & Engineering

Established in 1986, the department began with 60 students and now offers four autonomous programs:

  • B.E. (CSE)
  • B.E. (AI&DS)
  • B.E. CSE (AIML)
  • M.Tech. (CSE)

Recognized as a research center by VTU, the department specializes in areas such as Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, Cryptography, Cloud Computing, and more. It boasts multiple NBA accreditations and actively participates in cutting-edge research and development.

Department of Information Science & Engineering

Founded in 1999, the department has grown from 40 students to an intake of 120. It emphasizes experiential learning with state-of-the-art labs and a focus on emerging fields such as Data Science, Artificial Intelligence, and Cybersecurity. Research opportunities for Ph.D. and M.Sc. (Engg.) are available under VTU.

Department of Master of Computer Applications (MCA)

Established in 1994, the MCA program bridges theory and practice with a strong focus on software solutions, networking technologies, and multi-technology systems. Known for its strong placement record, the program nurtures industry-ready professionals with a blend of technical and soft skills training.
